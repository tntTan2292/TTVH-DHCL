## 15. P2 results (2026-11-05, Claude Code / Sonnet 5.5; for Opus review, not self-approved)

**Built (backend only, read only).**
- `backend/src/services/f13v2/CauseAggQueryService.js`: `summary`, `bcvh`, `routes`, `postmen`, `parcels` over `f13_cause_parcel`. One `WHERE` builder serves the figures and the parcel list (so a figure and its list cannot drift). Counts are integers; `shortfall_x10 = 9 N - 10 Đạt` (tenths of a parcel, exact); `own_shortfall` = `max(0, s)` and `net_contribution` = `s` of the group and period (never a sum of daily values); `rate` (percent, one decimal) is computed from the integers for display only; shares are unrounded ratios. Route level has the three scopes (each `POSTMAN` route, then the buckets `@NON_POSTMAN` and `@OTHER`); postman level groups by `ma_buu_ta_first`, no first postman = bucket `NONE` "Chưa có bưu tá". Cause mix over Không đạt only: the six causes, "chưa đủ căn cứ" as its own line with `by_step` and `by_flag`, `unclassified` (0 by invariant); hints counted beside. Every row carries `distinct_bg` and `repeat`; `meta.repeats = {rows, distinct, extra}`; `meta.basis = BG_DAY_ROWS`. Shares (`share_of_parent_shortfall` can be negative, `share_of_parent_deficits`, `share_of_parent_failed`) are inside the parent group; when the parent is on target the shortfall share is `null` with `share_note = KHONG_AP_DUNG`. `parents` (per BCVH) come from a query with the same filter, so distinct counts of a parent are true distinct counts, not sums of children.
- `meta.freshness` reports the **stored** day state only: `complete`, `stale_days`, `building_days`, `missing_days` (a day with `fact_f13` rows and no `f13_cause_day` row), `built_to`, `check = STORED_STATE`. The signature check is P3.
- Controller `F13V2Controller` (`getWhy*`, one shared `whyRequest`) and `f13v2Routes.js`: `GET /api/f13/v2/why/{summary,bcvh,routes,postmen,parcels}`, same middleware order as the other v2 routes (auth, role, `requireModule('f13')`, `scopeBcvhResponse`), not cached (the answer carries its own freshness).
